# CrowdCore
CrowdCore helps brands and agencies find creators whose audience and content actually fit the campaign. It combines creator search, audience and content-fit review, vetting signals, and format insights to turn discovery into shortlist-ready recommendations and briefs.

Product Features

- Search creators by niche, platform, audience, location, and content style
- Go beyond keyword matching to identify creators with stronger brand and buyer fit
- Review creator profiles, videos, captions, and comments in one workflow
- Analyze audience fit to see whether a creator’s followers match your target customer
- Evaluate content quality, style fit, and brand safety signals before outreach
- Identify proven formats, hooks, and product-integration patterns that already perform
- Rank creators based on campaign relevance, fit evidence, and potential risks
- Generate shortlist-ready recommendations instead of raw creator lists
- Turn creator research into campaign briefs, hooks, outreach angles, and action-ready reports
- Support both public creator discovery and private creator list / CRM vetting workflows
- Help brands and agencies move faster from creator search to campaign decision
- Provide structured creator intelligence that can also be used by internal teams or AI agents
